A simple homemade version. Adjust the chilli to your taste.

## Ingredients
- Carrots — 500 g
- Garlic — 3 cloves
- Neutral vegetable oil — 40 ml
- Vinegar, 9% acidity — 1 tbsp (15 ml)
- Sugar — 2 tsp
- Salt — ½ tsp, then more to taste
- Ground coriander — 1 tsp
- Ground black pepper — ¼ tsp
- Hot chilli powder — a small pinch

## Method
### 1. Prepare the carrots
Peel the carrots and grate into long, thin strips. Add the salt and sugar, gently massage with your hands and leave for 10 minutes.

### 2. Add the spices
Make a small well in the carrots. Put the minced garlic, coriander, black pepper and chilli in the well.

### 3. Dress the salad
Heat the oil in a small frying pan until hot but not smoking. Carefully pour it over the garlic and spices. Add the vinegar and mix well.

### 4. Let the flavours develop
Transfer to a covered container and refrigerate for at least 3 hours, preferably overnight. Stir and taste before serving; add a little salt if needed.

## A little tip
For a milder flavour, start with half the vinegar and add the rest to taste after chilling.
