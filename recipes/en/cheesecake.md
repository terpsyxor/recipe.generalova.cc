16–18 cm tin. The baked version from our earlier chat: no cream, with optional sour cream or thick plain yoghurt. Preparation time and servings are approximate.

## Ingredients
### For the base
- Biscuit crumbs — 160–180 g
- Butter — 65–75 g

### For the filling
- Cream cheese — 400 g
- Eggs — 2
- Sugar — 80–100 g
- Sour cream or thick plain yoghurt — 100 g (optional)
- Cornflour (cornstarch) — 1 level tbsp
- Vanilla — optional

## Method
### 1. Prepare the base
Mix the biscuit crumbs with the melted butter. Press firmly into a 16–18 cm tin. Bake for 8–10 minutes at 170 °C.

### 2. Mix the filling
Mix the cream cheese with the sugar. Add the eggs one at a time, stirring gently after each addition without whipping. Stir in the sour cream or yoghurt, if using, followed by the cornflour and vanilla. Pour the filling over the base.

### 3. Bake the cheesecake
Reduce the oven temperature to 150 °C. Bake for 45–60 minutes: the edges should be set and the centre should still wobble slightly.

### 4. Cool gradually
Switch off the oven, leave the door slightly open and let the cheesecake sit inside for 30–40 minutes. Then remove it and leave to cool.

### 5. Chill
Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight. Serve chilled.
