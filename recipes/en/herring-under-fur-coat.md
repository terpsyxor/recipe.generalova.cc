A simple homemade version with eggs. Use a salad bowl or an 18–20 cm cake ring.

## Ingredients
- Lightly salted boneless herring fillets — 250–300 g
- Potatoes — 3 medium (about 350 g)
- Carrots — 2 (about 200 g)
- Beetroot — 2 medium (about 400 g)
- Eggs — 3 (reserve one yolk for decoration if desired)
- Onion — ½ small
- Mayonnaise — 150–180 g, to taste
- Salt — if needed

## Method
### 1. Cook the vegetables and eggs
Boil the unpeeled potatoes and carrots until tender, about 20–30 minutes after the water comes to the boil, depending on their size. Cook the beetroot separately for 45–90 minutes, testing with a knife. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes after the water comes to the boil. Cool everything completely and peel.

### 2. Prepare the layers
Check the herring for any remaining bones and cut into small cubes. Finely chop the onion, cover with boiling water for 30 seconds, then drain through a sieve. Let all the water drain away, cool the onion and pat dry with kitchen paper: the salad does not need extra moisture. Coarsely grate each vegetable separately. If you want to decorate the salad, separate and reserve one cooked yolk in a covered container in the fridge. Grate all the whites together with the remaining yolks for the egg layer. If you do not want a garnish, grate all the eggs whole. Gently squeeze very juicy beetroot.

### 3. Assemble the salad
Build the salad from the bottom up. Use each ingredient in a single layer, spreading it evenly over the surface without pressing firmly. Add a little mayonnaise after each of the six layers. The herring and onion need only a very fine drizzle; reserve slightly more for the potatoes, carrots, eggs and beetroot topping. Use the listed 150–180 g for the whole salad so it does not become too rich.

1. **Potatoes.** Spread all the grated potatoes over the bottom and level gently. Taste the herring first: if it is only lightly salted, you may add a little salt to the potatoes; if it is salty, add none. Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise over the potatoes.
2. **Herring.** Spread all the diced herring evenly over the mayonnaise-covered potatoes. Drizzle with a very thin lattice of mayonnaise. Do not add salt.
3. **Onion.** Spread the scalded, cooled and dried onion over the herring. Drizzle with a very thin lattice of mayonnaise. Do not add salt.
4. **Carrots.** Spread all the grated carrots over the onion, level gently and cover with a thin layer of mayonnaise. No extra salt is needed.
5. **Eggs.** Spread the prepared grated whites and yolks over the mayonnaise-covered carrots, keeping the reserved garnish yolk aside. Level gently and cover with a thin layer of mayonnaise. Do not add salt.
6. **Beetroot.** Finish with all the grated beetroot, level gently and cover with the remaining mayonnaise in a thin, even layer. Do not add salt.

For easy spreading, drizzle the mayonnaise in a fine lattice, then gently spread it with a spoon without mixing the layers. Each layer is used once in this version.

### 4. Chill
Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight. If using a cake ring, remove it just before serving.

### 5. Decorate before serving
If you reserved a yolk, finely grate it over the mayonnaise-covered beetroot just before serving. Do not add mayonnaise over the yolk garnish. Decoration is optional: the salad is ready to serve without it too.

## A little tip
Do not pack the layers too firmly: a lighter touch makes a more delicate salad. Gently squeeze very juicy beetroot before assembling.
